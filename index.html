    How it grades

    The model reads your answer and copies out which sentences are the Situation, the Task, the Action and the Result. Then this page checks its work and does the counting. Three checks, one point each:

    • Structure. All four parts are there, setup is no more than 40% of the words, and the action is at least 30%.
    • Evidence. How it ended has a number in it, or a concrete outcome someone could check.
    • Length. One to two minutes out loud, at an assumed 150 words a minute.

    Three is Ready, two is Close, one or none is Rework. Every quote the model gives is checked against your answer, and a rewrite that uses a number you never wrote is thrown away.

    Your stories and your key stay in this browser. Your answer and your stories leave it only when you press Grade, and then only to Anthropic. If you use Speak, your browser's speech service hears the audio. This page has no server, no account and no tracking.

    This is practice. It can tell you whether an answer is clear. It can't tell you whether you'll get the job.
